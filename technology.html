Platform

Kelvin, and the machinery underneath it.

An agent is only as good as what it can reach. Kelvin sits on a task engine, six scientific models, and a lab it can actually operate.

Task engine

A goal in, a campaign out.

State the target and the constraints. The engine decomposes them into retrieval, generation, calculation, and screening tasks, schedules them, and re-plans when a result comes back wrong.

01Read

Literature, patents, and your own ELN resolved into structured, queryable facts.

02Hypothesize

Generative models propose candidates that satisfy every stated constraint at once.

03Simulate

DFT, MD, and surrogate models rank candidates before anyone touches a flask.

04Synthesize

Protocols dispatched to automated platforms, or to a chemist as an unambiguous recipe.

05Measure

Instruments report back in structured form, with provenance attached to every value.

Every measured result re-weights the next hypothesis

Scientific models

Six models, each answerable for one kind of question.

General language models reason about chemistry. These are trained to be correct about it — and to report when they are not confident.

Reaction
Whether a given step will go, under what conditions, and what else comes out of it.
Structure
Molecules and crystals generated and relaxed under the constraints you set.
Property
The mapping from a structure to the properties your specification actually names.
Spectra
NMR, XRD and MS read back into a structural assignment.
Protocol
Intent turned into a procedure a bench — or an instrument — can execute.
Literature
Every conclusion anchored to a citable source, with disagreement between sources kept.

Autonomous lab

The loop only closes if something moves.

Dry and wet lab under one orchestration layer: liquid handlers, reactors, and characterization instruments driven from the same plan that produced the hypothesis.

Orchestration

One scheduler across instrument vendors, with queueing, retries, and a full audit trail of what ran when.

LIMS & ELN integration

Results land in the systems your chemists already use, structured on the way in rather than cleaned up later.

Human checkpoints

Any step can be gated. Hazardous chemistry and irreversible spend always are.

Deployment & security

Your formulations never become someone else's training data.

Four ways to run it. Everything above this line is identical in all four.
ModeWhere the data sitsWho runs itTime to first result
APIYour infrastructure; only the call leaves it.YouSame day, once a key exists.
SaaSOur multi-tenant environment.UsFastest — no infrastructure to procure.
Private cloudYour VPC, your keys, your retention policy.Us, inside your accountWeeks — a normal cloud onboarding.
On-premisesNever leaves the plant.You, with our supportLongest — physical isolation is a project of its own.
Tenant isolation
Separate storage and a separate model context per tenant. Cross-tenant retrieval does not exist in the architecture.
Encryption and identity
Encrypted in transit and at rest. SSO and SCIM. A least-privilege key per integration.
Your data is not training data
Customer data is never used to train a shared model. That sentence goes in the contract.

Governance

Every number the agent reports can be traced to where it came from.

Provenance
Every value is labeled computed, measured or cited, and the label travels with it into your report.
Versioned knowledge
Models, datasets and protocols are versioned together, so a result from March can be reproduced in November.
Access control
Project-level roles and shareable dashboards, with an export path that respects them.
