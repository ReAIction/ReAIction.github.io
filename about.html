About

We are building the scientist, not the search engine.

ReAIction was founded on a narrow bet: that the bottleneck in materials R&D is not ideas, and not compute, but the number of times a team can complete a full loop from hypothesis to measurement.

Mission

Make a new material a decision, not a decade.

Vision

An AI scientist on every materials program on earth.

How we work

Three commitments we would rather lose a deal over.

We show you the results that contradict us

A model that only surfaces confirming evidence is worse than no model. Disagreement between sources stays visible in every answer.

We build for the hundred you actually make

Anyone can rank a million candidates. The hard part is the hundred you actually make, and we build for that end.

Chemists stay in charge

The agent proposes and executes. Judgement about what is worth trying, and what is too dangerous to try, stays with people.
